HOW IT WORKS

From last night’s stream to a finished reel, in four steps.

01 / 04  ·  RECORD

Record in OBS.

Record in OBS the way you always do, replay-buffer saves included. Then bring the videos in: drag them onto Home, or press Ctrl+I to browse.

  • Drop in as many videos as you like
  • Your own recordings are never touched

02 / 04  ·  ORGANIZE

Every clip, filed by day.

Home lays your clips out by the day they were captured, newest first: Today, Yesterday, then each date. Star the keepers, search the rest, and start a project from any clip or any whole day.

  • Projects can mix clips from any day
  • Binned clips wait in a Trash you can restore from

03 / 04  ·  EDIT

Cut it on a real timeline.

Video, photos, sound, titles and stickers sit on tracks and play together. Trim and split, change the speed, play a clip backwards, animate it with keyframes, and drop in a title from a ready-made style.

  • Picture effects, sound effects and presets, all in the Effects tab
  • Undo and redo, so you can try things

04 / 04  ·  EXPORT

Export for where it's going.

Pick a preset for TikTok, Shorts, Reels, YouTube or Discord, or set the format, frame rate and size yourself. Vertical 9:16 uses a smart crop that keeps the action in frame, and your original clips are never changed.

  • MP4 horizontal or vertical, MP3 audio only, or a GIF
  • Original, 24, 30, 60 or 120 fps · up to 4K

A LOOK INSIDE

Made for the dark, the way a stream is.

Footage first: the interface stays out of the picture's way, with one accent for what is live or selected and a quiet moving backdrop of glyphs behind it all.

SHORTS VIEW · SAFE ZONES DRAWN OVER THE PREVIEW · TEST FOOTAGE
THE STARTUP INTRO · THE BOLT, STRUCK IN GLYPHS
ASCII LOOK

The moving glyph background is part of the Studio. In Settings you can switch the ASCII look off, or choose how much of it shows through the panels.

DARK, OR LIGHT
Dark by default. Light and system themes when you want them.

Comfortable or Compact spacing, and an interface scale of 90, 100, 110 or 125%.

YOURS, UNTOUCHED
Editing never changes your recordings.

Everything you do is a trim, a layer or an export. A binned clip sits in the Trash until you empty it.

YOUR PC DOES THE WORK
Exports use your graphics card when they can.

If they can't, the processor takes over. Slower, same result.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS

Runs on an ordinary Windows PC.

Windows only for now. Everything the Studio needs is inside the installer.

GRAPHICS CARD
Optional. Exports use an NVIDIA card (driver 570 or newer) when they can, and the processor otherwise.
MEMORY
A 4K export of a long project needs a lot of memory. On a PC with 8 GB or less, export at Original or 1440p.

  1. 01Download ShipItStudio-1.2.15-Setup.exe and run it.
  2. 02Windows SmartScreen may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” box. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. 03Let it finish, then open Ship It Studio from the Start menu.
WHY THE WARNING

This is expected: the installer isn't code-signed yet, and SmartScreen warns about anything it hasn't seen signed. Some antivirus tools do the same on first launch. Allow it, and it should stop asking.
